### 1. The Crash: `AttributeError`

**The Cause:** During the transition from Alice to Bob, `wrapper.py` tries to reset a tracking flag called `bob_success_this_step` to `False` so it can monitor if Bob succeeds during his turn. However, this tensor was never created in the `__init__` method of your `EpisodeManager` class.

### 2. The Flood of Warnings: `[Lula] Joint ... is specified as a mimic joint`

**What this is:**
This is a non-fatal warning generated by Isaac Lab's underlying kinematics solver (Lula). You are using a Robotiq gripper (likely the 2F-85 or 140). These grippers use a "mimic joint" setup—when one knuckle moves, the other fingers automatically mimic that movement to close the hand.

Lula is complaining because your RMPflow/Lula configuration files (`lula_left.yaml` and `lula_right.yaml`) do not define these passive mimic joints as active "c-space" (configuration space) control coordinates.
